    The approval clock starts when a verified request reaches administrator review. Approval continues to the safety delay; denial cancels immediately; timeout closes the request. Users may choose a longer personal timeout.

  4. hourglass_top

    Safety delay

    Fine-tune in hours

    Personal preferences and existing delay floors may extend this wait. Playback resets the wait and requires fresh consent. Unaccepted requests expire after 30 days.

  5. auto_delete

    Verify & remove

    Recheck target, access, playback, consent and the delay before claiming one removal attempt.

    Deletion removes the movie or whole series and its files through Seerr. Request history is kept. Viewer consent and the deletion delay still apply.

    Uncertain outcomes stop for review; they are never automatically resent.
