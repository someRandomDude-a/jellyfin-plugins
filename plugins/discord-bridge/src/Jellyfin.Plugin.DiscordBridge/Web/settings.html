Account signup invitations — /invite

Users choose uses and expiry in the slash command, or use these defaults. Account Hub remains the signup owner and also enforces its template, creator delegation and quota. These Discord limits are editable here independently.

Private SyncPlay invitations — /watchparty

Uses your most recently active native playback device. The generated native link is posted in the current approved channel by default. Specify @user to send a DM instead. No Activity, embedded player, device-selection command or extra sharing confirmation is needed.
