Jellyfin watch-party companion

This Activity opens your party in Jellyfin, outside Discord. It does not play video inside Discord or grant media access.

Connecting to Discord…

Paste only a link from this server. Selecting Open invitation explicitly shares that bearer link with Discord's external-link command. Nothing is redeemed by this Activity, saved to browser storage or shown to other participants.

To create an invitation, use /link, open Jellyfin on your device, then use /watchparty devices and /watchparty invite. Linking and account recovery remain private self-service commands.
